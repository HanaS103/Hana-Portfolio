Electric Vehicle Adoption Trends in Washington State

Project Overview

This project analyzed electric vehicle (EV) adoption trends across Washington State using registration data. The goal was to identify growth patterns, compare vehicle types, and examine geographic trends related to electric vehicle ownership.

Project Objectives

  • Analyze EV adoption growth over time
  • Compare Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Vehicles (PHEVs)
  • Identify counties with the highest EV adoption
  • Explore utility and infrastructure relationships
  • Examine regional trends across Washington State

Dataset Overview

The dataset contained approximately 279,780 vehicle registration records and included information such as county, city, utility provider, model year, and vehicle type. Both BEV and PHEV vehicles were included in the analysis.

Data Preparation

  • Handled missing values
  • Verified duplicate records
  • Standardized categorical fields
  • Prepared data for visualization and trend analysis

Key Findings

  • EV registrations have increased rapidly in recent years
  • Battery Electric Vehicles dominate the market
  • Urban counties show significantly higher EV adoption
  • Infrastructure and utility access appear connected to EV growth

Recommendations

  • Expand charging infrastructure in underserved regions
  • Increase EV incentive programs
  • Support utility partnerships for charging access
  • Continue monitoring long-term EV growth trends

Skills Used

  • Research

What I Learned

Through this project, I learned how to analyze large datasets, identify growth patterns, create meaningful visualizations, and communicate findings through data-driven presentations.
