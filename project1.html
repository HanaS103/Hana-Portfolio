Project One Final Presentation

Project Overview

This project focused on analyzing crime trends in Los Angeles using real-world crime data. The project explored patterns involving crime frequency, victim demographics, geographic areas, and times of day when crimes most frequently occurred.

Project Team

  • Silvia Rosario Betances
  • Hana Sijad
  • Faith Wade
  • Vanessa Saum

My Role

My role in this project was Data Scientist. I helped analyze data, identify trends, and support the interpretation of crime statistics.

Main Questions Explored

  • What types of crimes are most frequently reported?
  • At what times do most crimes occur?
  • Which geographic areas report the most crime?
  • Has crime increased or decreased over time?
  • What victim demographics are most associated with crime?

Key Findings

The analysis showed that stolen vehicles were among the most commonly reported crimes. Crimes most frequently occurred during nighttime hours, and central Los Angeles reported the highest number of incidents.

The project also showed that crime overall decreased significantly between 2023 and 2024.
